This summer was one of the most memorable ones I've had in a long time. I spent the first few weeks relaxing at home, catching up on my favorite books and movies, but the highlight was definitely a two-week trip to the coast with my family. We stayed in a small cottage just a short walk from the beach, where my days were filled with swimming in the ocean, building sandcastles, and eating ice cream while watching the sunset. In the evenings, we played board games and told stories around a bonfire. It was the perfect mix of adventure and relaxation, and I returned home feeling refreshed and already looking forward to the next summer.
